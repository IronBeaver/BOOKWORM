## Bundled book distribution

The [Gutenberg permission guidance](https://www.gutenberg.org/policy/permission.html)
permits redistribution in many circumstances and explains the distinction between
US public domain, trademark conditions and other countries. This preparation
retains the complete book, source link, credits, notices and embedded license.
The app/project is intended to be free. Before a public release, the owner must
check applicable local redistribution law and the full Gutenberg terms, especially
for a paid or differently branded distribution. MIT permission for BOOKWORM code
does not waive Gutenberg conditions or license third-party book content worldwide.

The audit found no additional bundled runtime dependency, font or decorative
artwork requiring another application license. It does not claim rights in the
underlying book, character mappings, system fonts or external APIs. Changes to
assets/dependencies require another review rather than silently extending MIT.

Sources: [MIT text](https://opensource.org/license/mit),
[Gradle8.9 upstream license](https://github.com/gradle/gradle/blob/v8.9.0/LICENSE),
and the complete notices already included with the EPUB.
